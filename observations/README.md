## Working conclusions (descriptive only)

1. **Number of addressee in imperative** tracks **genre** more than a single “language has / lacks 2PL” switch (LT clear; SL has both in one corpus).
2. **Dual** is not a yes/no for the whole Freising text: paired-body dual appears; verbal dual does not in this slice.
3. **be / uk / sa body forms** are blocked by access or OCR quality — recorded as gates, not as negative linguistic results.
