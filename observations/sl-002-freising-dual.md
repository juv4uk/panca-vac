## Question

Is dual **entirely absent** from the Freising critical slice, or only absent in some categories (e.g. imperative)?

sl-001 recorded dual **imperative** as `UNATTESTED IN SLICE`. This pass checks dual more broadly on the same live text.

## Attested — dual of paired body-part “eyes”

Fixed liturgical/judgment formula “before (X’s) eyes”:

| EXACT_FORM (critical layer) | Location | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| `pred bosima ozima` | II | before God’s eyes |
| `pred bosima osima` | II | same formula (variant spelling in critical text) |
| `pred Tuoima osima` | III | before Your eyes |

CONTEXT: judgment / standing before God (`stati pred … osima`).

GRAMMATICAL_FORM: dual instrumental of the paired noun “eye” (CS-type *očima* pattern), with agreeing dual modifiers `bosima` / `Tuoima`.

This is **not** verbal dual and **not** imperative dual. It is nominal dual in a frozen high-register formula.

## Unattested in this slice

| Category | Status |
|----------|--------|
| Dual imperative | `UNATTESTED IN SLICE` (confirms sl-001) |
| Dual of non-paired nouns (productive) | not identified in this pass |
| Dual verbs (1DU/2DU/3DU finite) | not identified in this pass |

Absence type for verbal/imperative dual: **sampling / genre** — short penitential texts; dual imperative is rare in IE generally. Do **not** upgrade to “early Slovenian has no dual.”
