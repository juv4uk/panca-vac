## Relation to probe 001 / lt-001

lt-001 (Vilnius prayers) showed **only 2SG** imperative, absence of 2PL classified as **text-contingent** (Pater/Ave/Creed have no plural human addressee).

lt-002 tests that classification: a longer LT print from the same century with explicit plural address should surface 2PL if the language has the category in this register.

**Result: 2PL is productively attested.** The lt-001 absence was genre-bound, not structural.
