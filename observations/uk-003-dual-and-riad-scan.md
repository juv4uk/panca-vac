## Dual — attested inventory (apparatus + text)

Chepiha’s grammatical sketch (OCR) states that the monument **preserves some dual forms**, while also showing replacement by plural with *два/оба*.

### Noun dual (examples cited by edition)

| Form (edition OCR) | Leaf (as cited) | Reading |
|--------------------|-----------------|--------|
| `двіма господинома` | 37 | dat./instr. dual “two masters” |
| `полінома` | 85 | dual |
| `сь дв(х)манадєсл(т)ма оучєникома` | 110 зв. | “with the twelve disciples” dual morphology |
| `дві рибі`, `дві роуці`, `дві нозі` | various | nom./acc. dual with *дві* |
| `очи` / `очима`, `оуши` / `ошима` | various | paired body-part dual |

Gospel narrative OCR (Matthew zone) also shows:

```text
жє(т) двіма господинома, слоужити
```

(same collocation as in the sketch).

### Edition’s own caution

> even with numerals *два*, *оба* the plural often replaces dual  
> (`два гроші`, `два моужі`, …) — evidence of **weakening** of dual in 16th-c. book language.

So dual is **attested but not robust** as a full living paradigm in this witness.

## «ряд-» scan (this pass)

Hits for *ряд* in the OCR are dominated by:

- *рядок* / *рядків* (line of text),
- *поряд* / *порядковий* (next to / ordinal),
- modern scholarly *ряд* (“a number of …”).

**No clear EXACT_FORM** of legal/covenant *рядъ / рядь* (treaty, ordinance) was isolated in this pass from the gospel body or the index snippet scanned.

Status for legal *ряд-*: **NOT FOUND IN THIS PASS** (not “absent from the language”).

A dedicated pass over the **словопокажчик** lemmas under Р- would be the next check if that probe is locked.
