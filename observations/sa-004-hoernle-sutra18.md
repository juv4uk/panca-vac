## Form sample (Hoernle OCR, lightly spaced)

```text
Sutram |
Adyor vishesadvigunam chayashuddhi vibhajitam
… gatisasyam tada bhavet ||
```

Labeled by Hoernle as **18th Sutra** (progression / time of meeting).

English gist (Hoernle): twice the difference of initial terms, divided by difference of increments, plus one → time.
