## What this opens

Unlike Ṛgveda-as-composition rhetoric (sa-001), Bakhshālī is a **physical surviving mathematical witness** with:

- named find-spot and repository;
- published transliteration tradition (Kaye; Sarasvati/Jyotishmati on IA; Hayashi critical);
- content domain: arithmetic / rules / examples (sutras), not Vedic liturgy.

## What is NOT claimed here

- No EXACT_FORM paradigm for dual / imperative (math register ≠ Freising prayer grid).
- No inheritance link to Slavic *ряд-* from place-value or series vocabulary without separate probe lock + forms.
- IA OCR must not be pasted as clean Sanskrit without check against Hayashi/Kaye plates.
