## Attested forms (preface, biblia.by orthography)

Didactic 2SG-style address to the reader (“you who want to learn…”):

| EXACT_FORM (transcript layer) | CONTEXT |
|---|---|
| `чти ее` | read.IMP the Psalter |
| `чти книгу светого Іова` | read.IMP the book of Job |
| `чти книги Саломоновы` | read.IMP Solomon’s books |
| `часто чти` | read.IMP often (Numbers / arithmetic) |

Also modal / future-leaning reader address:

| EXACT_FORM | CONTEXT |
|---|---|
| `Хощеши ли умети...` | if you wish to know grammar… |
| `знайдеши в зуполной Бивлии Псалтыру` | you will find the Psalter… |

GRAMMATICAL_FORM: imperative 2SG (and 2SG-oriented didactic address), **reader** addressee, not God / not plural congregation.

```text

языком, что которое слово знаменуеть
```

→ explicit bilingual layer (CS body + Ruthenian glosses). Relevant for contact vs inheritance discipline later; **not** an imperative-number fact.
